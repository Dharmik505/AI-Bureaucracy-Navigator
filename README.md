# AI Bureaucracy Navigator

An intelligent web application designed to help Indian citizens discover government subsidies, grants, and welfare schemes they are eligible for based on their profile.

## 🎯 Project Overview

The AI Bureaucracy Navigator is a chatbot-powered platform that simplifies the process of finding relevant government schemes. Instead of navigating complex government websites, users answer simple questions about their income, employment status, category, state, and family situation to receive personalized recommendations.

## 🌟 Features

✨ **Interactive Questionnaire**
- Simple 6-question form covering key eligibility criteria
- Dropdown selections for ease of use
- Income-based filtering

✨ **Smart Matching Algorithm**
- Matches user profile against 12+ major government schemes
- Considers income limits, employment status, and category
- Real-time result generation

✨ **Comprehensive Scheme Database**
- Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

- Ayushman Bharat

✨ **Beautiful UI**
- Modern gradient design

## 📊 Included Government Schemes

### Business & Employment
- **PMMY**: Up to Rs. 10 lakh loans without collateral
- **Startup India**: Funding and mentoring for startups
- **PMEGP**: Subsidized loans for enterprises
- **PMKVY**: Free skill development programs

### Financial Inclusion
- **PMJDY**: Zero-balance bank accounts
- **PMSBY**: Accident insurance (Rs. 2 lakh for Rs. 12/year)
- **PMJJBY**: Life insurance (Rs. 2 lakh for Rs. 436/year)
- **APY**: Pension scheme (Rs. 1000-5000/month from age 60)

### Healthcare & Social Security
- **Ayushman Bharat**: Free health insurance (Rs. 5 lakhs/year)

### Housing
- **PMAY**: Subsidized housing loans

### Agriculture
- **PM-Kisan**: Rs. 6,000/year for farmers
- **PMFBY**: Crop insurance protection

## 💻 Usage

1. **Fill the Form**: Answer the 6 questions about your profile
   - Annual Household Income
   - Employment Status
   - Category (General/SC-ST/OBC/Minority)
   - State
   - Age Group
   - Children Status

2. **Click 'Find My Schemes'**: Submit your information

3. **View Results**: Get personalized scheme recommendations

4. **Learn More**: Click on any scheme to view details
